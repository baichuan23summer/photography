# Sample photograph sources

The active `.webp` files in `public/photos/sample/` were generated as neutral demonstration content for this starter portfolio. They are not presented as documentary records or as Baichuan Peng's finished work.

## Generated assets

Mode: built-in image generation, one image per prompt. The outputs were saved into this project as WebP files without cropping or visual effects.

Condensed prompt set (all use a natural, photorealistic editorial style; no text, logos, watermarks, borders, or collages):

- `public/photos/sample/coast.webp`: a California coastal path disappearing into morning fog, landscape 3:2.
- `public/photos/sample/bus.webp`: an anonymous passenger through a rain-covered bus window at dusk, portrait 2:3.
- `public/photos/sample/breakfast.webp`: tea cups, plates, and a folded newspaper left after breakfast, square.
- `public/photos/sample/gas-station.webp`: an empty gas station reflected in wet pavement at night, landscape 3:2.
- `public/photos/sample/dumplings.webp`: two generations of hands folding dumplings together, landscape 4:3.
- `public/photos/sample/stairwell.webp`: palm shadows crossing a concrete stairwell, portrait 2:3.
- `public/photos/sample/fog-road.webp`: a rural road and bare tree disappearing into winter fog, landscape 16:9.
- `public/photos/sample/curtain-portrait.webp`: an anonymous young East Asian adult behind a translucent curtain, portrait 4:5.
